KVP Partners runs this site. This policy explains, as the Personal Information Protection Act of Korea requires, what personal information the site handles and how.

## What we handle, why and for how long

| What | Why | How long |
|:-----|:----|:---------|
| Reactions under articles: the emoji and a random id made by your browser (no name, no IP address) | To count reactions and let you undo your own | Until you remove the reaction |
| A scrambled (hashed) form of your IP address that changes every day | To stop floods of reactions and problem reports | One minute (reactions) or one day (reports), then deleted automatically |
| Problem reports from the Visa map and Visa docs pages: your message, your name and contact if you give them, your choices on the page with a picture of it, and your browser type | To fix the page and reply to you | Until the problem is fixed, then deleted |

Your answers on the Visa map and Visa docs pages stay in your browser. They are kept in the page address so you can save or share your map, and they reach us only if you send a problem report. Reports never include sensitive answers: Korean descent, and the yes/no questions on Visa docs. Please don't write health, religion or criminal-record details in a report.

Sending a report is optional: without it you can use every page as usual. If you are under 14, please ask a parent to send it for you.

## Who else handles it

- **Cloudflare, Inc.** (USA) hosts the site and its small server, including the reactions database. Like any web host, its servers around the world see your IP address to deliver the pages.
- **Telegram:** a problem report is delivered to the KVP Partners team as a Telegram message, over an encrypted connection, when you press **Send report**. Telegram keeps it on its servers outside Korea until we delete it.
- Articles with comments show Telegram's comment widget, loaded from telegram.org. What you post there is handled by Telegram under its own privacy policy.

We don't sell or give your information to anyone else, and we don't use it for advertising.

## In your browser

The site sets no cookies of its own. It keeps the random reaction id and your light or dark mode choice in your browser's storage; clearing this site's data in your browser removes them.

## Your rights

You can ask us to show, correct or delete a problem report you sent, or to stop using it. Reactions carry no name, so we can't tell which are yours; remove a reaction with the same button.

## Contact

Privacy questions and requests: the KVP Partners team, [help@kvppartners.com](mailto:help@kvppartners.com).

You can also turn to the Personal Information Infringement Report Center ([privacy.kisa.or.kr](https://privacy.kisa.or.kr), call 118) or the Personal Information Dispute Mediation Committee ([kopico.go.kr](https://www.kopico.go.kr), 1833-6972).
