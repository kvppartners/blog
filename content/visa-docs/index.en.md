## How it works

1. Choose your nationality and your current visa, or "No visa yet".
2. Choose the visa you want: type a code or a word, or pick it from the list. Your visa's usual next steps come first, then every other visa with an official document list (✈ means you apply from abroad; ✈* means from abroad, or in Korea in the cases the manual allows: choose under **How do you apply?**). To stay longer on your current visa instead, tick **Extend my current visa**.
3. If you like, open **Make the list yours** and answer the questions. They come from the official list itself: its applicant cases, the extra documents it asks for some occupations and regional programmes, and the documents it marks as "if applicable". Cases that apply only to some nationalities, such as visa agreements, are shown only to them. Without answers you see every document, with a note on who needs it.
4. Download the list as a picture.

Each document shows its Korean name, so you can ask for it in Korean.

## Important

The lists are copied from the Korea Immigration Service manuals (September 2026 edition); the source is shown under each list. For a few visas the manual names only part of the documents, and the page says so. The immigration office or the embassy can ask for more documents in your case, and the rules change, so check with [HiKorea](https://www.hikorea.go.kr) or the immigration call centre **1345** before you apply, or contact us for a free consultation.
