## How to use

1. Find the form: type its name, its Korean name or a visa code, or pick a group.
2. Download it as **PDF** to print and fill in by hand, as **HWP** (Hangul, the format Korean government offices use) or as **Word**. Some forms have only some of these.
{{% document-sources %}}
3. On the **Visa docs** page, a document that is one of these forms links here.
{{% /document-sources %}}

## Important

The forms are copied unchanged from official Korean government websites on 5 October 2026; the source is named above each group. Forms change from time to time, so before you submit one, check on the source page that it is still the current version. This archive only collects blank forms. KVP Partners does not fill in or submit forms for anyone.

## Sources

- Ministry of Justice, Korea Immigration Service: [HiKorea, forms (민원서식)](https://www.hikorea.go.kr/board/BoardApplicationListR.pt)
